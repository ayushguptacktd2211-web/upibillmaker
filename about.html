Our Story & Vision

About UPIBillMaker

Empowering Indian micro-merchants, freelancers, doctors, tutors, and small business owners with instant, 100% free digital invoicing backed by NPCI UPI instant auto-pay technology.

Why We Built UPIBillMaker

In India, millions of retail shops, service technicians, private coaching tutors, and freelance consultants issue cash memos and estimates on physical paper slips or complex paid software like Tally, Zoho, or Vyapar. Many of these platforms impose steep monthly subscriptions, complex accounting hurdles, or watermark your invoices.

We created UPIBillMaker.com to solve this fundamental problem: providing a lightning-fast, zero-registration, completely client-side invoice and receipt generator that anyone can use directly on their smartphone, tablet, or desktop in under 30 seconds.

100% Client-Side Privacy

Zero database storage. Your customer names, phone numbers, item rates, and GSTIN stay strictly inside your device's browser.

Instant Auto-Pay QR

Compliant with NPCI Unified Payments Interface standard. Customers scan the QR to pay exact amounts via GPay, PhonePe, or Paytm.

Free Forever Guarantee

No mandatory signups, no watermarks, no trial limits, and no paid tiers. High-definition A4 PDF & PNG downloads are 100% free.

Compliance & Regulatory Standards

All invoices generated by UPIBillMaker are designed to adhere to:

  • Section 31 of Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017: Accurate display of Tax Invoice headings, supplier GSTIN, recipient details, HSN/SAC codes, and CGST/SGST/IGST tax breakdowns.
  • National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) UPI Protocol: Certified URL scheme formats (`upi://pay?pa=...&pn=...&am=...&tn=...`) ensuring universal compatibility across 100+ Indian banking apps.
  • Section 10(13A) of the Income Tax Act: Standard rent receipts accepted by corporate HR departments for House Rent Allowance (HRA) tax exemption filings.
  • Section 80C Education Allowance: Compliant tuition fee receipts for school and coaching tax deductions.
