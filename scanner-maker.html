How to Use the Free UPI Scanner Maker

Print a high-quality acrylic counter display or sticker for your business in 3 steps.

1. Choose Amount Mode

Pick "Any Amount" if you want customers to type their bill amount, or choose "Fixed Amount" to lock in ticket/plate/consultation fees (e.g. ₹50, ₹100, ₹500).

2. Upload Shop Logo

Add your business logo and choose your favorite standee color theme (PhonePe Purple, GPay Blue, or Merchant Green).

3. Print & Stick

Download as high-res PNG sticker or print directly on A4/card paper to place in a standard acrylic T-stand on your cash counter.
