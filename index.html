How to Create a Valid GST Tax Invoice with UPI QR

Compliant with Indian Goods and Services Tax (GST) invoicing guidelines.

1

Input GSTIN & HSN Codes

Enter your 15-digit GSTIN and state. For B2B customers, enter their GSTIN to allow them to claim Input Tax Credit (ITC).

2

Automatic CGST & SGST Split

The calculator automatically splits 18% GST into 9% CGST + 9% SGST for intra-state sales, or applies 18% IGST for interstate customers.

3

Generate Auto-Pay QR Code

The grand total including taxes is encoded into an NPCI dynamic QR code. Customers scan from GPay, PhonePe, or Paytm and settle instantly.

GST Invoicing Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between CGST, SGST, and IGST?

When you sell goods or services to a customer in the same state (Intra-state), GST is split equally between Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST). When selling to a customer in another state (Inter-state), Integrated GST (IGST) is applied.

What happens if I don't have a GST number?

Simply uncheck the "Enable Official GST Details" box! The tool immediately switches to Non-GST Cash Memo mode, allowing tutors, small shops, and freelancers to create clean, simple receipts without GST fields.
