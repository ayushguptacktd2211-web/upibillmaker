Legal Knowledge for Indian Business

Kacha Bill vs Pakka Bill: What is the Legal Difference under GST?

Understanding the legal difference between an Estimate / Non-GST Cash Memo (Kacha Bill) and an official GST Tax Invoice (Pakka Bill) is essential for every business.

Feature Non-GST / Estimate Memo (Kacha Bill) Official GST Tax Invoice (Pakka Bill)
Legal Status Internal sales record and cash memo only. Limited legal admissibility for commercial audits. 100% legally recognized document under Section 31 of CGST Act, 2017.
Tax Breakdown (GST Split) No GST added (0% tax or base commercial value only). CGST + SGST (intra-state) or IGST (inter-state) clearly itemized.
Input Tax Credit (ITC) Buyer cannot claim input tax credit (ITC). Registered B2B buyers can claim 100% ITC on their GST portal.
Warranty & Insurance Major brands and insurance providers may reject claims without official invoices. Completely valid for manufacturer warranties, corporate expense claims, and insurance.
Who Can Issue? Unregistered small traders, freelance tutors, home service providers below threshold. All GST-registered businesses exceeding annual statutory turnover thresholds.
Is it legal to issue invoices without a GST number?

Yes. If your annual business turnover is below the statutory GST registration threshold (₹40 Lakhs for goods in most states, ₹20 Lakhs for services), you can legally issue a "Non-GST Cash Memo" or "Bill of Supply" without a GSTIN. On UPIBillMaker, simply uncheck "Enable Official GST Details" to generate a legally compliant Non-GST cash memo.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about online GST invoicing, Non-GST receipts, UPI QR codes, and income tax compliance.

Q1. Is this online bill generator 100% free with no watermark?

Yes! UPIBillMaker is 100% free with no subscription fees, no sign-up or mobile number registration, and no mandatory watermark on your downloaded PDF or PNG receipts.

Q2. Can non-GST registered businesses make bills without a GST number?

Yes. Simply uncheck the "Enable Official GST Details" checkbox in the form. The tool immediately transforms into a Non-GST Cash Memo mode, allowing small kirana stores, tutors, and freelancers to create clean, simple receipts without GST fields.

Q3. How does the Auto-Pay UPI QR code work?

When you enter your UPI VPA ID (e.g. yourname@okaxis or shop@ybl), the tool encodes your VPA and the calculated grand total into an NPCI-compliant UPI payment intent string. When the customer scans the QR code from Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM, the payee name and exact bill amount are already filled in.

Q4. Can I claim Income Tax HRA exemption using this rent receipt?

Yes! Select the "Rent (HRA)" tab. The receipt is formatted strictly in compliance with Section 10(13A) of the Income Tax Act, 1961. It includes landlord PAN (mandatory if annual rent exceeds ₹1,00,000), rental property address, tenancy period, and official declaration.

Q5. What is the difference between CGST, SGST, and IGST?

When selling goods or services to a customer located within the same state (Intra-state sales), GST is split equally into Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST)—for example, an 18% GST rate becomes 9% CGST + 9% SGST. When selling to a customer in another state (Inter-state sales), Integrated GST (IGST) is applied at the full 18%.

Q6. Can I print on an 80mm thermal receipt printer?

Yes! In the Restaurant tab, choose the "Thermal POS Slip (80mm Narrow)" format. It arranges items, quantities, rates, and the QR code into a centered vertical roll layout designed specifically for standard 80mm POS receipt printers.

Q7. Is my business and customer data safe? Can anyone access my records? (Top Security)

All your records stay strictly within your own device browser's Local Storage. No Cloud Upload • Zero Tracking • 100% Private & Secure.

Q8. Can I send the bill directly to customers on WhatsApp?

Yes. Click the green "WhatsApp" button on the preview tool bar. It formats an instant billing summary message with invoice number, amount, payment link, and opens WhatsApp Web or your phone's WhatsApp app with 1 click.
